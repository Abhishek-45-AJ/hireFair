4. Adversarial Debiasing Model Architecture

HireFair utilizes a PyTorch Neural Network trained with a Gradient Reversal Layer (GRL):

  • Shared Feature Encoder ($E$): Learns latent candidate embeddings from skills and text similarity.
  • Qualification Predictor ($P$): Optimizes standard Binary Cross Entropy ($\mathcal{L}_{task}$) for job fit prediction.
  • Adversarial Gender Detector ($A$): Tries to detect sensitive gender attribute $S$ from the latent space.
  • Gradient Reversal Layer (GRL): During backpropagation, GRL multiplies gradients from $A$ by $-\alpha$, forcing $E$ to unlearn gender cues while preserving qualification signals.
